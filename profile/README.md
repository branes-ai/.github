# Branes.ai

### Building the compute foundations for intelligent machines.

Branes.ai is working at the intersection of processor architecture, computing systems, and robot autonomy. We believe the next generation of intelligent machines will require computing platforms designed specifically for the demanding workloads of autonomous systems.

Our mission is to help make that future possible through innovative processor architectures, software infrastructure, and developer tools.

## What We Do

Autonomous machines place unique demands on computing hardware. Real-time decision-making, sensor processing, perception, and planning require architectures that balance performance, power efficiency, and predictable execution.

We are developing the foundations for this next generation of computing:

- **Processor Architecture** — Designing computing architectures tailored to the needs of autonomous systems.
- **Software Infrastructure** — Building the tools, compilers, and software foundations that make specialized hardware accessible to developers.
- **Robotics & Autonomy** — Enabling the demanding computational workloads behind intelligent, autonomous machines.
- **Developer Ecosystem** — Creating an open and accessible environment for building, experimenting, and innovating.

## Open Source

We believe open-source software is essential to advancing computing technology.

Our GitHub organization is where we share selected projects, tools, libraries, and experiments with the broader engineering community.

We welcome collaboration from developers, researchers, and engineers who share our interest in computer architecture, high-performance computing, and autonomous systems.
